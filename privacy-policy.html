Data Collection

Current storage: calculator inputs are by default held only in the memory of the open page and discarded on close or reload. A history entry with an optional note is created only when you explicitly save it and is kept locally in the browser (not automatically transmitted to the operator). Settings such as market or theme are stored only after your explicit choice. Voluntarily enabled storage: only if you enable "Remember inputs on this device" is the input draft (without the note) stored in LocalStorage. Possible legacy data: earlier versions could store inputs automatically and write markers for a start animation, an install-prompt visit counter and a lastSaveTime timestamp; these functions no longer exist, and existing legacy data is not deleted or converted without your say-so.

When you share a calculation or export the history, contents may reach a recipient or storage location you choose as text, an image or a file. A history backup also includes saved notes. This is an action you initiate. New website history links carry the entry identifier in the URL fragment (after #), which browsers do not send to the server; historical links with id, entryKey or entryIndex parameters remain supported and may send these identifiers to the hosting provider. The links do not embed calculator values or notes in the URL.

Offline mode is voluntary: the website registers a Service Worker only after your action "Make available offline", and "Remove offline mode" removes the registration and this website's caches. Clearing that cache alone removes cached assets. Clearing all website data may also delete localStorage containing saved calculations and remembered settings. The hosting and country-based redirects described in the full policy apply to the website and installed PWA. Native iOS storage and export are described below.

You can use the calculator without creating an account.

There is no start animation and no visit counter any more; the install help is an instruction page you open yourself and stores no choice.

After activation, pages and static resources may be stored in the browser's cache; identifiers of history links are not stored in cache keys, and calculator inputs and notes are not written to the cache. A Service Worker registered automatically by an earlier version, and its cache, may continue to exist until removed.

Native iOS App

The native iOS app loads its bundled pages and resources locally in a WebView. Saved calculations, notes and settings are held in local WebView storage in the app container, separately from the browser's website data. Starting these local contents does not use the website's hosting, Service Worker or country-based redirect. External links and sharing destinations you choose may use network connections.

Imported JSON backups are processed locally. For a JSON or CSV export, the app writes a temporary file to its cache directory (CACHE) and opens the native share dialog. You choose the recipient or storage location. After the sharing attempt, the app tries to delete its temporary file. If cleanup is interrupted or fails, the file may remain. Copies already shared or saved elsewhere are not removed by this cleanup or by deleting the app's history.

Hosting

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When accessed online, technically necessary connection data (in particular IP address, user agent, timestamp, request URL and referrer, if sent by the browser) are processed by the hosting provider to deliver the website, defend against attacks and ensure service stability. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure operation of the website).

Cloudflare’s terms incorporate a data processing addendum for covered customer data. Cloudflare identifies customer logs and content transmitted through its network as examples of processing on a customer’s behalf. Cloudflare may act as controller for its own purposes.

Cloudflare may process operational records to deliver and protect the service. The country redirect function does not write logs of its own. We have no verified fixed deletion period covering all provider request records for this site. Cloudflare describes retention criteria including purpose and legal obligations.

On a static Cloudflare test page, the NEL and Report-To headers supplied a policy that supporting browsers may retain for up to seven days. On connection failures the browser may send a report to a.nel.cloudflare.com. The reporting rate for successful requests was zero in the test. Reports may include the requested URL and its parameters, the referrer and error details. Cloudflare says it uses the client IP to identify network characteristics and does not log it in this reporting pipeline. For evspendpure.com this function is switched off in the domain settings.

Cloudflare operates a global network and also processes personal data outside the EU and EEA, including in the United States; Cloudflare also uses sub-processors in several countries. When this website is accessed online, this concerns technical request data such as the IP address. This does not automatically transmit locally stored calculations or notes to Cloudflare.

Cloudflare states in its Privacy Policy that it is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF), the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. DPF and the Swiss-U.S. DPF.

For transfers not covered by the DPF, Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum provides for the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Modules Two and Three) with the supplements for the United Kingdom and Switzerland.
